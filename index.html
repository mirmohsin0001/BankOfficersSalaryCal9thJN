Those drawing basic pay up to the 8th stage of the clerical scale are fitted at the minimum of the Officers' scale (Scale I: ₹48,480) and draw their next increment on the anniversary date of promotion. Those higher up are fitted per the clerical-to-officer fitment table, which is not in the Joint Note provided, so it is not calculated here.
